# Marketing Analysis — HomeOffer Direct

_Owner: Marketing Analyst · Status: v1 · Last updated: 2026-06-12_

The Marketing Analyst's charter: position the product, define the audience and
channels, craft compliant messaging, and own the acquisition funnel. This brief
builds on the business case in
[`../business/business-analysis.md`](../business/business-analysis.md) and the
product spec in [`../product/prd.md`](../product/prd.md).

> **Compliance is a hard gate, not a footnote.** Every message here must pass the
> Fair Housing gate ([`../legal/fha-messaging-gate.md`](../legal/fha-messaging-gate.md))
> and the UPL gate ([`../legal/upl-compliance-gate.md`](../legal/upl-compliance-gate.md)):
> no targeting/excluding by protected class, no "love-letter" personal appeals,
> no legal advice — only educational guidance and the savings story.

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner.** *Buy your home without a buyer's agent — and keep the commission.*

**Positioning statement.** For confident US home buyers who don't want to give up
~2.5% of the price to a buyer's agent, HomeOffer Direct is a guided, state-aware
workflow that walks them from search to closing — so they capture the commission
savings themselves, with the guardrails to do it safely. Unlike FSBO portals
(seller-side) or generic real-estate marketplaces (agent-funneling), we are the
**buyer's** transactional co-pilot.

**Core promise / proof.** Pay a small flat fee to help capture ~$10k of savings
(~30× ROI for the buyer) — backed by the savings calculator, the offer builder's
concession ask, state-by-state legal guidance, and loud trust callouts.

## 2. Audience (ICP & segments)

Segments are defined by **behavior and intent only** — never demographics or any
protected class (FHA gate).

| Segment | Intent signal | Hook |
|---------|---------------|------|
| **Confident repeat buyers** | "I've done this before; why pay an agent again?" | Keep the commission; we handle the paperwork timeline |
| **Finance-savvy first-timers** | Researching, spreadsheet-driven, fee-averse | Plain-English Journey + savings math |
| **Cash / investor buyers** | Speed, no financing contingency, repeatable | Offer builder + tracker + deal export |
| **FSBO-adjacent / direct deals** | Already negotiating directly with a seller | State legal engine + document binder |

## 3. Messaging pillars

1. **Keep your commission.** The ~2.5% is negotiable and yours to capture — but
   only if you ask. (Quantified by the savings calculator.)
2. **You're not alone in it.** Guided 14-stage Journey, state-aware legal path,
   and handoffs to real attorneys/inspectors/title — confidence, not bravado.
3. **Safe by design.** Wire-fraud, the Closing Disclosure 3-day rule, and the
   final walkthrough surfaced loudly; educational guidance, never legal advice.
4. **Honest and on your side.** Flat fee, no commission incentive, no
   pay-to-play referrals, privacy-first.

Tone: clear, calm, empowering, jargon-free. Avoid hype and anything implying
guaranteed outcomes or legal/financial advice.

## 4. Channels & strategy

| Channel | Play | Priority |
|---------|------|----------|
| **Organic search / content SEO** | Evergreen guides mapped to the Journey + glossary + 50-state legal pages — high-intent long-tail ("how to buy a house without a realtor in <state>") | **P0** |
| **Programmatic state pages** | The state legal engine already yields 51 SEO-rich landing pages — optimize titles/meta and interlink | **P0** |
| **Tools as lead magnets** | Savings calculator + offer/affordability tools rank and convert; gate advanced output behind sign-up | P1 |
| **YouTube / short-form** | "Watch me buy a house agent-free" educational series | P1 |
| **Community** | r/RealEstate, r/FirstTimeHomeBuyer, FSBO forums — helpful, non-spammy presence | P1 |
| **Partnerships (B2B2C)** | Lenders, FSBO platforms, fintechs bundling self-directed buyers | P2 |
| **Paid search (selective)** | Bid on high-intent agent-free / commission-savings terms once CAC is proven | P2 |

Rationale: the product's structural advantage is **content depth + 51 state
pages + interactive tools** — an SEO-and-tools flywheel is the most defensible,
lowest-CAC engine. Paid comes after organic proves intent and unit economics.

## 5. Funnel & metrics

Mirrors the Business Analyst's funnel so marketing and business share one
scoreboard:

| Stage | Marketing metric | Target lever |
|-------|------------------|--------------|
| Acquisition | Organic sessions, share of non-brand traffic, CAC | Content + state-page SEO |
| Activation | Journey starts, savings-calc completions | On-page CTAs, tool prominence |
| Conversion | Paid-unlock rate from tool users | Value framing (30× ROI), packaging |
| Advocacy | Referrals, cloud-sync sign-ups, NPS | Post-close prompts, share/export |

North-star alignment: marketing optimizes for **qualified buyers who reach the
offer builder**, since that's where realized savings (the company north-star)
are created.

## 6. Competitive framing

- **Buyer's agents / brokerages** — our anti-incumbent; we convert their fee into
  the buyer's savings. Message respectfully: agents add value for some; we serve
  those who choose to self-represent.
- **FSBO portals (Zillow FSBO, etc.)** — seller-side; we're buyer-side and
  transactional, not just a listing surface.
- **Real-estate marketplaces** — monetize by routing buyers *to* agents; we're
  the opposite.
- **DIY legal / forms sites** — generic; we're home-purchase-specific and
  state-aware, and we stay on the right side of the UPL line.

## 7. Launch plan (first 2 sprints)

1. **SEO foundation:** audit/optimize titles, meta, and structured data on the
   Journey, glossary, and all 51 state pages; build an internal-linking hub.
2. **Cornerstone content:** publish "Buy a house without an agent: the complete
   guide" + per-state variants, each routing to the savings calculator.
3. **Conversion copy:** apply the **keep-your-commission / 30× ROI** framing to
   the landing page and the savings-calculator result, with a clear unlock CTA.
4. **Measurement:** stand up privacy-first analytics for the funnel above (no
   protected-class data, ever) and a weekly marketing↔business review.
5. **Compliance pass:** run all new copy through the FHA + UPL gates before ship.
